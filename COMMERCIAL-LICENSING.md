The GPL asks one thing in return for the freedom it grants: if you distribute this
software, or a work based on it, you pass the same freedom on and make your
corresponding source available under the same licence. For most people that costs
nothing and nothing further is required.

A commercial licence exists for those it does not suit. It releases you from the
copyleft terms for the parts Agenxy wrote, so you may ship them inside a product whose
source you do not publish. Enquiries go to Agenxy.

## What it covers

It covers the Agenxy-authored parts of this repository, which Agenxy owns and may
therefore license on other terms.

It does not cover third-party dependencies. Those are licensed by the people who wrote
them, on terms Agenxy cannot alter, and a commercial licence here grants you nothing
with respect to them. Their terms are permissive, which is why they can be distributed
inside a closed product at all, and keeping to them remains your responsibility.

## Why this is possible

Dual licensing works only while one party holds the rights to everything it relicenses.
Agenxy holds the copyright in everything in this repository today. Outside contributions
would change that, which is why [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) asks future contributors
for a licence agreement or an assignment. Without one, a contribution could be
distributed under the GPL and never under a commercial licence, and the two would drift
apart until the commercial licence described something that no longer existed.
